COMBATE MECÁNICO
_____________________
(Robo War)
RULES AND REGULATIONS:
• Students of classes IX to XII can participate.
• Team size: 2 members per team.
• The competition will be held in 3 rounds: Prelims, Semi-Final and Final.
• Robot dimensions must not exceed 40 cm × 40 cm × 40 cm. There is no
tolerance on dimensions.
• Robot weight must not exceed 5 kg. There is no tolerance on weight.
• The controller will not be included in the weight calculation.
• Robots can be wired or wireless.
• Weapons are allowed. However, cutting blades and flames are strictly
not allowed.
• Robots will be checked for dimensions and weight before the
competition. Robots not meeting the limits will be disqualified.
• If a robot develops an error during the competition, the team can opt
for a repair time of 2 minutes.
• Points will be awarded on the basis of aggression, as described in the
judgement criteria.
• The decision of the judges will be final and binding on all teams.
• Each match will be of 3 minutes.
Points will be awarded on the basis of aggression, i.e:
~ Immobilizing the opponent's robot
~ Damaging the opponent's robot
~ Flipping the opponent's robot
~ Pushing the opponent's robot out of the arena
