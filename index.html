01/ABOUT

THE FUTURE
AWAKENS

ESPERANZA is a celebration of creativity, knowledge, and youthful excellence, bringing together students in an enriching platform of learning, expression, and healthy competition.

Rooted in the spirit of collaboration and discovery, the event encourages students to explore their talents, strengthen their confidence, develop essential skills, and learn beyond the boundaries of the classroom.

With a diverse range of engaging events, Esperanza aims to inspire curiosity, foster camaraderie, and empower young minds to discover their potential while celebrating the joy of learning together.

RULES & PROTOCOL

⏱ TIMING & REGISTRATION

  • Event date: 6 November 2026
  • Participants must be present for registration at 8:00 AM. Registration closes at 9:00 AM.
  • The registration fee is ₹120 per participant.
  • The fee and participants' list are to be deposited on 28 October 2026.
  • Eligibility: This event is only for students of Std VI to XII. Eligibility varies by event. Please check the individual event details.
  • One student can participate in only one event.
  • A maximum of one team per school can participate in team/group events.
  • All participants must be accompanied by a teacher or moderator.
  • Last date of registration: 28 October 2026.
  • Registration link: https://forms.gle/Hg6dUBVKkBPj6jUY6

⚡ CODE OF CONDUCT

  • Dress Code: The dress code for all participants is black jeans and a white shirt.
  • Anonymity: The name or other specifications of the participants should not be revealed under any circumstances.
  • Team Codes: A team code will be allotted to each school after registration, which will be used for all further courses of action.
  • Device Policy: No mobile devices or internet will be allowed during the competition, except for designated photography/photoshoot events.
  • Discipline: Any indiscipline in behaviour may lead to the disqualification of the participants.
  • Judging: Judges' decision will be final and binding.
